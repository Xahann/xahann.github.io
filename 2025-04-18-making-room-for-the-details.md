---
title: Making room for the details
date: 2025-04-18
category: Process
description: Why the smallest decisions often do the most work.
---

The details are rarely just details. They’re the moments where a person decides whether a thing feels clear, useful, and made with care.

That doesn’t mean adding more. Often it means taking something away: one less step, one clearer sentence, one small decision that helps the whole experience make sense.

I try to leave a little room in every project for those decisions. The result is usually quieter—and more human.
