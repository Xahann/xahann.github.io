GRID ("we," "our," or "us") is designed with a strict privacy-first architecture. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user information when you use the GRID mobile application (the "App").

## 1. The Core Principle: Zero Data Collection

GRID does not collect, transmit, store, track, or share any personal information or usage data whatsoever.

**No Accounts:** You do not need to register, log in, or provide an email, phone number, or name.

**No Network Activity:** GRID operates entirely offline. The App has zero network capabilities, does not connect to external servers, and does not make internet requests.

**No Third-Party SDKs or Trackers:** GRID includes no third-party software development kits (SDKs), advertising networks, analytics platforms, or telemetry frameworks.

**No Ads:** The App does not display advertisements or participate in ad exchanges.

**No Payments or Billing:** The App does not collect financial data, credit card information, or process in-app purchases.

## 2. Device Permissions and Photo Processing

To edit photos, GRID requires access to your device’s photo library or camera.

**On-Device Processing:** All image rendering, editing, filters, adjustments, and metadata modifications take place strictly on your local device hardware.

**Storage:** Edited photos remain stored locally on your device. We have no access to your image library, files, or edited outputs.

**No Remote Uploads:** Your photos never leave your device through GRID.

## 3. Children’s Privacy

GRID does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age in your jurisdiction). Because the App operates entirely offline and collects no data, it complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## 4. Third-Party App Stores

While GRID itself collects no data, your download and installation of the App are governed by the platform operator through which you obtained it (e.g., Apple App Store, Google Play Store). These platforms operate under their own independent privacy terms and data practices.

## 5. Changes to This Policy

Because GRID has no online infrastructure or communication channels, updates to this Privacy Policy will only occur via application updates. Any changes will be posted directly within this document and accompanied by an updated "Last Updated" date.
