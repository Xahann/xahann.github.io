---
title: A good place to begin
date: 2025-02-06
category: Notes
description: Every worthwhile project starts with a better question.
---

Before the moodboards and milestones, I like to ask one thing: what would make this genuinely better for the person on the other side?

It’s a simple question, but it has a way of bringing a project back to what matters. The answer becomes a useful compass for everything that follows.
