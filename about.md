I’m **Xane**, a photographer, videographer, and editor focused on artists, live music, and car culture.

I work across photography and video to capture people, performances, and the details around them. My portfolio includes artist features, concert coverage, and automotive work.

### What I do

- **Photography** — artist portraits, live events, and automotive work.
- **Videography** — music and event coverage.
- **Editing** — shaping footage into finished pieces.

See more work at [photos.xane.live](https://photos.xane.live/) or connect on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/xane.photography/).
